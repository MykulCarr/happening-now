About this page

A dashboard for what's happening right now — pulling headlines straight from a curated set of newsrooms. Use the scope chips below to switch between Local, Regional, National, and International views. The top ribbon scrolls a fresh feed of breaking headlines while the cards below load the latest stories from each source. National defaults include NPR, The Guardian (US), The Atlantic, PBS NewsHour, Ars Technica, and Hacker News. International draws from BBC, The Guardian (World), Al Jazeera, Deutsche Welle, and Nature. Set your location from the topbar to unlock Local and Regional scopes — those tap into local TV stations, newspapers, and Reddit communities for your city and state.
