Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Overview

HAPPENING NOW is a personal dashboard that runs in your browser. We designed it to minimize data collection.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores your settings and lightweight cached content in browser localStorage so pages load faster and remember your preferences (theme, ZIP code, watchlist, widgets, and similar dashboard settings).

Data Sent To Third Parties

When you use dashboard features, your browser requests data from third-party providers, including weather APIs, news feeds, market data providers, and NASA/public space-data services. Your IP address and request metadata may be visible to those providers as part of normal web requests.

Weather Map Embed

The interactive weather map on the Weather page is embedded from Windy.com. Because it is an embedded frame, loading it shares your approximate location, IP address and referring page with Windy, under their own privacy policy. It does not load until you ask for it — the map area shows a prompt with a Load map button, and nothing is requested from Windy until you click it. You can tick "Always load without asking" to skip the prompt; that choice is stored only in the browser you tick it in, and clearing site data resets it.

RSS Proxy

News requests and source icons pass through a Cloudflare Worker proxy on this domain. As well as solving cross-origin restrictions, this means publishers, market-data providers and the icon service see the proxy rather than you: the proxy sends its own request headers and does not forward your IP address or referring page to them.

Cookies And Tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies or behavioral profiling scripts. Cloudflare Web Analytics or Real User Measurement may be enabled to collect aggregated, privacy-preserving traffic and performance data for site operations. Some third-party embeds or external links you open may use their own cookies under their own policies.

Google Analytics is loaded with Google Consent Mode set to deny storage by default, and this site never asks for or grants that consent. As a result Google Analytics writes no cookies and stores no identifier on your device, in any region. That is why you are not shown a cookie banner.

Analytics Providers

When enabled, Cloudflare analytics measures page visits, referrers, basic performance signals, and similar operational metrics. Based on current configuration, analytics collection may be limited or excluded for some visitors, including visitors in the EU.

This site also uses Google Analytics 4. Because storage consent is permanently denied, it receives only cookieless measurement pings — aggregate page views, referrer, approximate geography derived from your IP, and general device or browser type. Your visits cannot be linked together across sessions, and no advertising or personalization signals are sent. Google processes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Device Location (GPS)

The app never requests your device's GPS position automatically. Location access is only initiated when you explicitly click a GPS button in Settings, and only after you grant permission in your browser's permission prompt. You may deny or revoke that permission at any time in your browser settings. If you use the ZIP or city lookup instead, no GPS request is made.

Data Retention

Locally stored settings and caches stay in your browser until they expire naturally or you clear site data/reset settings.
