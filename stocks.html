About this page

Track the markets at a glance. The ticker above runs quotes for a starter set of benchmarks — Dow, S&P 500, NASDAQ, Russell 2000, gold and bitcoin — each tagged with current session status; another seventeen indices, commodities and currencies can be switched on in Settings. Your watchlist below lists every symbol you're following, with sortable pinning and current quotes. Market News pulls headlines for your watchlist or major outlets, alongside Top Gainers, Top Losers and Trending stocks. An Economic Calendar of upcoming earnings, Federal Reserve announcements and economic data releases is available in Settings too. Configure your watchlist and which indices appear there.
